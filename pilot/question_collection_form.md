# Question collection form (copy into Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or print)

**Intro text:** We are testing a BSCS study chatbot. Please type the questions you would REALLY
ask a study helper, exactly as you would type them (typos, Filipino or Taglish are fine).
Do not include your name or personal details. Your answers are used only to improve the chatbot.

**Questions (short answer, all optional):**
1. Think of the subject you are studying this term. Write 3 questions you wish you could ask a tutor about it.
2. Write 2 questions you searched for online while studying recently.
3. Write 2 questions you were too shy to ask in class.
4. Write 1 exam-review question you would ask the night before a quiz.
5. Write 1 question that has nothing to do with computer science (we want to make sure the bot refuses it politely).

**Target:** 5 or more students, 20-30 distinct questions in total, covering at least 5 different subjects.
Paste the answers into `pilot/collected_questions.csv` (one question per row), then follow
`pilot/PILOT_GUIDE.md`, step 5.
